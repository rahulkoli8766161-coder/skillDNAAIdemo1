How do you handle scaling & optimization in high-concurrency systems?

Model Answer Strategy:

Explain caching strategies (Redis/Memcached), asynchronous queue workers (Celery/RabbitMQ), database indexing & read-replicas, load balancing, and microservices decoupling.

What is your methodology for debugging complex production failures?

Model Answer Strategy:

Demonstrate structured diagnostic thinking: check centralized APM metrics & stack trace logs, reproduce minimal test cases, isolate state mutations, inspect upstream dependencies, write patch unit tests, and deploy post-mortem prevention.

Explain the difference between Monolithic vs Microservices Architecture.

Model Answer Strategy:

Monoliths offer single-codebase deployment simplicity, lower operational overhead, and easy ACID transactions. Microservices offer independent service scaling, technological flexibility, and domain isolation at the cost of distributed networking complexity.
